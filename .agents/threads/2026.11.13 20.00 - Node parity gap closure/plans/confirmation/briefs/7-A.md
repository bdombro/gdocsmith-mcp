2. **Lens alignment (`paragraphPut`, opt-in only).**
   - Let `visible` be the ascending model indices of `!symHidden` symbols, with V = visible.size(). Gap g (0..V) is the hidden symbols between `visible[g-1]` and `visible[g]`. Gap 0 is before `visible[0]`; gap V is after `visible[V-1]`.
   - Pair and diff in visible coordinates. Keep `source` and the range-marker logic (l.316–334) in visible coordinates.
   - The model splice for a visible hunk [aStart, aEnd) follows the original `modelMap` exactly:
     - **end** = `aEnd == 0 ? 0 : visible[aEnd-1] + 1`. That's just after the last visible symbol before gap aEnd, so before gap aEnd's hidden symbols.
     - **start** = `aEnd > aStart ? visible[aStart] : end`. For a replacement or deletion, that's after gap aStart's hidden symbols. For a pure insertion it's equal to end.
     - The new text replaces model [start, end).
   - Consequences:
     - **Pure insertion at gap g:** the inserted text lands before every hidden symbol already in gap g. That includes g = 0, where it's placed before leading hidden symbols, and g = V, before trailing ones.
     - **Replacement or deletion:** gap aStart's hidden symbols stay before the new text, and gap aEnd's hidden symbols stay after it. Hidden symbols in interior gaps aStart+1..aEnd-1 are removed by the lens, and the classifier then refuses them (hidden in a replaced range).
